# Pilot scorecard — first 3 Reels

For each real clip, record:

| Metric | Suggested initial target |
|---|---|
| Human planning time | Under 20 minutes per Reel |
| Manual corrections after render | At most 2 rounds |
| Caption accuracy | 100% verified by a Georgian speaker |
| Technical checks | All green |
| Copyright/consent | Explicit approval recorded |
| Social outcome | Track 3-second hold rate, watch-through and inquiries (if accessible) |

Targets above are experimental internal goals, **not guarantees**. Three videos provide qualitative feasibility feedback, not statistical proof of improved marketing outcomes.

## Experiment content formats
1. 15s aesthetic-center room tour / mood with only verified facilities.
2. 20s professional introduction (staff consent, verified credentials).
3. 20s educational 'common question' without diagnosing clients or promising efficacy.

Keep everything equal where possible (platform, ratio, duration), but do not overinterpret performance data from three posts.
