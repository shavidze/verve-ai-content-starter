# Brand style and safety
Read the job's `brand_profile` (Verve: `brands/verve.json`) first. Use its colors and brand tone; do not fabricate a specific business address, service, result, pricing, promotion or client statement. Use Georgian captions only if transcript is verified. For treatment and injection footage, avoid implying guaranteed or risk-free results, and never use misleading before/after imagery. Obtain appropriate consent from recognizable clients/staff. People and raw footage stay local unless owner has expressly authorized an external provider.

## Caption position (owner rule, always)
Captions always go at the BOTTOM of the frame, in the lower third, above the platform UI (brand `caption.margin_bottom`, default 400). Never place captions above the head or at the top of the frame, even if a held prop is partly covered. If a per-job tweak is needed, only `caption_style.margin_bottom` between 330 and 480 is allowed. Top-of-frame space is for the single overlay badge, not captions.
