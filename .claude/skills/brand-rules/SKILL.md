# Brand style and safety
Read `brands/example-esthetic-center.json` first. Use its colors and brand tone; do not fabricate a specific business address, service, result, pricing, promotion or client statement. Use Georgian captions only if transcript is verified. For treatment and injection footage, avoid implying guaranteed or risk-free results, and never use misleading before/after imagery. Obtain appropriate consent from recognizable clients/staff. People and raw footage stay local unless owner has expressly authorized an external provider.
